INSTRUCTIONS FOR USE OF FORM 60260-6
INSTRUCTIONS FOR BOARD OF SURVEY

Boards of survey will:

  1. Find equipment submitted serviceable or not as the case may be.
  2. Recommend as to the disposal of articles condemned whether they should be—
    (a) sold
    (b) sent to centre for parts to be utilised
    (c) order that they should be destroyed
    (d) transfer.
  3. The proceedings of the board should be in triplicate. One copy will if approved by the head of department be send for retention by officer-in-charge.
INSTRUCTIONS FOR OFFICER-IN-CHARGE OF MOVABLE ASSETS
  1. Certificate of destruction given by officer-in-charge on how destroyed.
  2. In case of sale, cash should be properly brought to account, supported by the proceedings of the board.
  3. In case of transfer a report when dispatched and where to should be rendered.

Losses should be submitted to each BOS and explanations.
